JARVIS

J.A.R.V.I.S for automating daily ops — brain + face.

AI operations agent with brain-and-face architecture. Terminal 1 — the brain (npm run bridge), Terminal 2 — the face (Vite at localhost:5173). Opens in Chrome, click INITIALISE, say "Hey Jarvis". Double-clap welcome flow, Spotify, Chrome windows, ElevenLabs voice. Planned to orchestrate DelQuro Connect, VMTA, Megatory Live, and ChoreMouse with MCP allowlist, sub-agents, propose → execute-with-approval → fully autonomous.
