DelQuro Connect

Your veterinary team, aligned every shift.

DelQuro Connect is a communication and coordination app built for veterinary hospital teams that need one dependable place to stay aligned across every shift. Huddle feed for announcements, real-time chat with photos and read receipts, posted schedules the whole team can reference, and Time Off requests with quick manager approval. Role-based RBAC, Team Kudos, no sprawl.
