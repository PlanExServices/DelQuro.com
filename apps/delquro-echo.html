What Echo does

Inbox help that doesn’t trade your privacy.

  • ✓
    Thread summariesLong back-and-forths become 3 bullets: what happened, what’s needed, by when.
  • ✓
    Drafts in your voiceEcho learns your tone from sent mail — concise, warm, direct — not generic AI-speak.
  • ✓
    Triage, not just filtersNeeds reply today, FYI, waiting on others — sorted without rules spaghetti.
  • ✓
    Local-firstModels run on-device. Your mail never leaves unless you explicitly send a draft.

How Echo stays private

Privacy is architecture, not a toggle.

  • ✓
    On-device modelsSummarization and drafting happen locally — no inbox sync to a vendor cloud.
  • ✓
    Zero exfiltration by defaultNothing is uploaded for training. No hidden retention.
  • ✓
    You control what leavesOnly the email you hit send on leaves your device. Drafts stay local until you decide.
  • ✓
    AuditableOpen logs of what was summarized and when — no black box.
Why Echo

Inbox that respects your boundaries.

Most AI inbox tools want full access to your mail in their cloud. Echo flips it: the AI comes to your inbox, not the other way around.

02 / Fast

03 / You

Sound like you, not a bot

Drafts mirror your past sent mail — short, warm, to the point.
