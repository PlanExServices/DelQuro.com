What adults get

Clarity without pretending you are fourteen.

  • ✓
    Kid & teen slang → plain EnglishPaste a word, a phrase, or a whole baffling message. Get a clear meaning in normal adult language.
  • ✓
    Built for parents and caregiversNot a trend dictionary for clout — a decoder so you can follow what your kids are actually saying.
  • ✓
    Vibe & contextSilly flex, friendly tease, outdated, or “maybe ask a follow-up” — know the temperature before you react.
  • ✓
    Conversation, not surveillance theaterUse translations to stay involved and talk — not to perform slang you do not mean.

How a decode goes

From “what did they just say?” to “okay, I get it.”

  • ✓
    Paste what you heardOne word, a text, a caption, a gaming chat line.
  • ✓
    Read the plain versionMeaning first. Examples when they help. No essay unless you want one.
  • ✓
    Check the vibeIs this goofy, affectionate, rude, or something to gently ask about?
  • ✓
    Respond like a grown-upStay in your own voice. Optionally grab a plain reply. Skip the cringe cosplay.
Why PlainSlang exists

Kids’ language moves. Adults still need the map.

You should not need a teenager on retainer to understand dinner-table talk, group chats, or the caption under a video. PlainSlang is that map — for adults.

01 / Understanding

Stop nodding and guessing

Know what “no cap,” “rizz,” or the new word of the week meant in that message.

02 / Calm

React to the real meaning

Fewer overreactions to harmless slang. Clearer eyes when something actually needs a talk.

03 / Connection

Stay in the conversation

Meet kids where their words are — without pretending the slang is yours.
