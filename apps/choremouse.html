Why families stick with it

Less arguing. More crumbs collected.

ChoreMouse is not a chart on the fridge that everyone ignores by Wednesday. It is a tiny game with grown-up guardrails.

01 / Motivation

Kids actually open it

Coins, streaks, and a shop they care about beat a paper checklist every time — without turning your house into an arcade.

02 / Trust

Parents stay the boss

Nothing pays out until you approve it. Rewards are ones you chose. Allowance stays fair because the rules are visible.

03 / Peace

One shared scoreboard

Fewer “I forgot” moments. Fewer debates about who did what. One place the whole family can point to.
