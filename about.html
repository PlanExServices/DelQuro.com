[01] — Vision & Mission

Calm software that stays useful.

We build a durable library, not a single breakout hit. Every volume is engineered to be quietly indispensable.

VISION

A library that gets more useful the longer it stays

23 volumes today across Family, Veterinary, AI, Inventory, Create, Sleep, Build & Learn, and Studio — including live products Megatory Live, VMTA, S.O.L.A.R., and PlainSlang at their own domains. Each volume earns its place on the shelf through real use, not feature count.

MISSION

Solve one real problem well

Start with friction in real days — family kitchens, vet front desks, stockrooms — and build only what clarifies the task. No third-party ad SDKs, no personal data sold, payments handled by App Store / Play / Stripe / RevenueCat so we never store full card numbers. VMTA triage data auto-purges in 24h by design.

HOW WE SHIP

Small, focused, reliable

React Native + Expo on the front, TypeScript, FastAPI, MongoDB behind it, PWA and on-device AI where it genuinely helps. Cloudflare privacy-respecting analytics. Tight feedback s, quiet maintenance.

[02] — Values

What we build by.

These are the same principles you see on every product page — our build protocol and studio values.

01 / REAL NEEDS

Start with the day

Every volume begins with real-life friction — where people lose time, context, or confidence. If it doesn't come from a real day, it doesn't become a product.

02 / RESTRAINT

Build what earns its place

Every screen and feature has a job. If it doesn't make the experience clearer, it doesn't ship. Small on purpose beats big on promise.

03 / CARE

Refine in the real world

Real use is the test. We listen closely, keep the tight, and make each release more dependable. Calm, reliable, quietly indispensable.

Privacy & Trust

0 trackers sold to anyone. Parent-managed family products (ChoreMouse) are COPPA-aware, no ads for kids. No ad network, no behavioral tracking. You can request deletion anytime via account-deletion.html or info@delquro.com.

[03] — Leadership

William DelQuro — CEO

Founder and studio lead. Product direction, design restraint, engineering culture.

Founder · Product direction · Virginia

William leads DelQuro Labs, an independent product studio based in Virginia building a living library of small, focused apps. Under his direction, the studio maintains 23 volumes across seven shelves, with 4 live on the open web today.

He sets the build protocol that shapes every volume: real needs first, restraint in scope, and care in refinement. That keeps each product small on purpose, with clear privacy practices and a bias toward reliability over feature count.

Day to day, he focuses on product direction, design review, and engineering culture — React Native and Expo, TypeScript, FastAPI, MongoDB, PWA, and on-device AI where it genuinely helps.
