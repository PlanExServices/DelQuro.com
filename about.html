Founder · Product direction · Virginia

William leads DelQuro Labs, an independent product studio building a living library of small, focused apps. The studio is based in Virginia and operates under a single principle: software should solve one real problem well.

Under his direction, DelQuro Labs designs, builds, and maintains 23 volumes across seven shelves — Family, Veterinary, AI, Inventory, Create, Sleep, Build & Learn, and Studio. Four are live on the open web today: Megatory Live at megatory-live.delqurolabs.app, VMTA at vmta.delqurolabs.app, S.O.L.A.R. at solar.delquro.com, and PlainSlang at plainslang.com. The rest remain on the lab bench until they graduate.

He defines the studio's build protocol, which is visible in every product page:

01 / Real needs — start with friction observed in real days, not imagined use cases. Family meal planning, veterinary shift handoffs, inventory counts done on a phone in a stockroom.
02 / Restraint — build only what earns its place. If a screen or feature doesn't make the experience clearer, it doesn't ship.
03 / Care — refine through real-world use. Tight feedback loops, quiet maintenance, and a bias toward reliability over feature count.

This approach keeps each volume small on purpose. There are no third-party ad SDKs in the apps, no personal data sold to anyone, and VMTA triage data is designed to auto-purge on a 24-hour cycle. Payments are handled by the platform you buy through — App Store, Google Play, Stripe, or RevenueCat — so the studio never sees or stores full card numbers.

Day to day, William focuses on product direction, design review, and the studio's engineering culture — React Native and Expo, TypeScript, FastAPI, MongoDB, PWA, and on-device AI where it genuinely helps. The goal is not a single breakout hit, but a durable, calm library that gets more useful the longer it stays on the shelf.
