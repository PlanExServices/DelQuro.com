🌿 Note Thyme

Real family meal plans, powered by real recipes.

Note Thyme is the mobile meal planner that stops guessing. Set your family's ages, allergies, favorite cuisines and cook-time limits once, and Thyme searches for real recipes from publishers including Allrecipes, Serious Eats, Budget Bytes, Minimalist Baker and others, then links you to the publisher’s original recipe. Results are filtered against your allergens and presented using US-imperial units and your choice of °F or °C. Discover ephemerally, save what you love, and build a week in seconds with lockable meals, skips, rerolls and cuisine focus like “3 Mexican dinners this week”. Every planned meal opens to real step-by-step instructions, scales to any number of servings, and rolls into an auto-generated grocery list. No made-up meals, no clutter, no chaos.

🐭 ChoreMouse

Chores kids love, allowance parents trust.

ChoreMouse turns family chores into a game kids actually enjoy. Parents assign chores from 120+ ready-made ideas or create their own, set point values, and approve completed work — with optional photo proof. Kids log in to a playful portal where they earn coins, climb the weekly leaderboard, keep daily streaks alive, unlock badges, and spend coins in your family's Treat Shop. Add multiple kid profiles with avatars, PINs, and weekly goals, and invite a co-parent to help. Free to download with a 7-day trial, then ChoreMouse Plus unlocks everything. Safe by design: parent-managed, no ads, no kid data selling, COPPA-minded, and full account deletion built in. Build good habits, together — one crumb at a time.

✦ DelQuro Connect

Your veterinary team, aligned every shift.

DelQuro Connect is a communication and coordination app built for veterinary hospital teams that need one dependable place to stay aligned across every shift. It brings the day's essentials together: a Huddle feed for announcements and manager updates, real-time team Chat with photos and read receipts, posted Schedules the whole team can reference, and Time Off requests with quick manager approval. Role-based access keeps admins, managers, and staff in the right lanes, while WAG's keeps morale high. No feature sprawl — just clear, practical tools worth opening every morning to keep the hospital running smoothly.
