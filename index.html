04 / A small, focused studio

Good software starts
with a real need.

DelQuro Labs is an independent software studio in Virginia. We build around everyday tasks: understanding a new phrase, learning how AI works, organizing a family routine, or helping a veterinary team.

Some tools are available now. Others are public experiments or works in progress. We keep their stages clear so you know what to expect.

Before you explore

A few useful answers.

Visit product support ↗
Which DelQuro apps can I use now?

PlainSlang and AI Quest are available. Other public projects carry a preview, clinic-access, or in-development label. Check the product page for its current release note.

What does “public preview” mean?

A preview lets you explore work that is still being developed. It may use sample data, have incomplete features, or store progress only in your browser. Read the limitations on each product card before using it.

How do veterinary teams get access?

DelQuro Connect and VetForm GO are arranged through participating clinics. Contact the studio about availability. Pet owners receive form links directly from their clinic.
